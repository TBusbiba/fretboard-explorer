            Chords in “Random” (arpeggios)

            An arpeggio runs one chord tone per string from the root's string (6, 5 or 4) up to string 1, within five frets.

            Triads

            Major and minor triads on three adjacent strings, played low to high: any of the three close shapes counts. Custom picks major or minor and the string groups; Path works through the levels.

            Scales

            Play the scale in the box you are shown, up and back down. Each note counts as soon as it is heard, even while the last one still rings. Wrong notes are soft: a red mark, no voice. Very fast runs, hammer-ons and slides may be missed.
